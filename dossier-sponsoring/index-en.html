Introduction

A word from the club

Dear Sir or Madam, dear partners,

Since 2015, Union Sport Aourir (Ittihad Aourir) has brought the children and young people of Aourir together around one passion: football. Every week, our coaches train six age groups, from U11 to U18, as well as our beach football section.

Beyond results, the club is a place to learn respect and solidarity. Tournaments, end-of-season ceremonies, snacks for the children: US Aourir keeps sport alive at the heart of the town.

For the 2026/2027 season, we invite the region’s companies, hotels, shops and institutions to associate their brand with a sporting and educational project, and to gain real visibility with families, supporters and visitors to the Agadir area.

This proposal presents our club, our needs and our three partnership packages. We would be delighted to meet you.

01 · Who we are

The club

Union Sport Aourir – Ittihad Aourir, founded in 2015, is a football club based in Aourir, just north of Agadir, between the mountains and the ocean. It trains the town’s young players through a supervised football school, competitions and events open to families.

A crest that tells Aourir’s story

The crest brings together the golden mountain, the waves and the ball: our bond with a region between land and sea. Its colours, green, gold and white, run through our whole identity, from the jerseys to social media.

Our values

  • Respect for teammates, opponents and referees
  • Team spirit: improving and winning together
  • Discipline: commitment, effort and rigour
  • Solidarity: the club as a family, serving the neighbourhood
